# Finance Tracker - Smart Wallet Manager 🚀

Manage your money with ease using a sleek, modern, and intuitive financial companion. This app is designed to help you stay on top of your spending, reach your savings goals, and understand your financial habits at a glance.

## ✨ What this app offers:

### 🧮 Interactive Arithmetic Calculator in Nominal Inputs
Say goodbye to external calculator apps when logging transactions.
- **In-Line Math Calculations**: Type basic arithmetic expressions (`+`, `-`, `*`, `x`, `:`, `/`, `()`) directly into the Nominal field (e.g., `24.000 - 7.000` or `1.500.000 : 3`).
- **Live Result Preview**: Real-time evaluation displays the calculated total (e.g., `= Rp 17.000`) right below the input box as you type.
- **Real-Time Thousands Separators**: Expressions automatically format number segments with thousands separators in real-time (e.g., `24.000 - 7.000`).
- **Numeric Phone Keypad**: Nominal input automatically opens a dedicated numeric/phone keypad with numbers `0-9` and operators `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` for seamless typing.

### 📌 Redesigned Recent Transaction Pinning
Organize your recent transactions on the homepage with a cleaner, hold-to-pin interaction.
- **Hold-to-Pin Confirmation**: Long-press any recent transaction in **Transaksi Terkini** to prompt a confirmation dialog before pinning or unpinning.
- **Clean UI**: Unpinned transactions no longer display cluttered pin icons. Pinned transactions are highlighted with a prominent blue/teal pin symbol and float to the top of your list (up to 3 pinned items).
- **Persistent Pin State**: Pins persist securely across app launches and clean up automatically if a pinned transaction is deleted.

### 🎯 Flexible 'Tabungan' (Savings Goals) & Deposits
Don't just save—save with a purpose. Create specific savings targets and track your progress in real-time.
- **Nominal or Percentage Deposits**: Deposit or withdraw funds either by typing a custom nominal value (Rp) or selecting a percentage (%) of the balance in your chosen payment method.
- **Quick Preset Chips**: Tap `10%`, `25%`, `50%`, `75%`, or `100%` chips for quick deposits.
- **Money Distribution Integration**: Savings deposits and withdrawals directly update source payment method balances in your Money Distribution overview.

### 💰 Monthly Budgeting & Expense Limits
Take control of your spending habits with the Category-Based Budgeting system.
- **Smart Limits**: Set monthly spending limits for each category (e.g., Food, Transport) using either fixed Rupiah amounts or percentages of your balance.
- **Over-Budget Warnings**: Get instant popup notifications if an expense exceeds your set limit, showing exactly how much you've gone over.
- **Visual Progress**: Track your spending in real-time with dynamic progress bars that accurately reflect your monthly usage.
- **Daily Spending Averages**: Stay aware of your habits with the **Rata-rata Pengeluaran Harian** feature. See your overall daily average and deep-dive into specific categories.

### 📊 Comprehensive Financial Analysis
Understand your financial health with clear, interactive visualizations on the **Keuangan** page.
- **Cash Flow Chart**: Compare your Income vs. Expenses visually to see your monthly surplus or deficit.
- **Detailed Rekap**: Get a breakdown of your total Income, Savings, and Expense for any given month.
- **Category Distribution**: See exactly where your money goes with detailed charts and transaction detail.

### 💳 Money Distribution & Privacy
Keep track of your assets across different platforms like **Cash**, **Wondr**, **Dana**, **GoPay**, and more.
- **Always Visible Charts**: See your wallet distribution at a glance on the homepage.
- **Privacy First**: Exact amounts are masked by default and can be toggled with a single tap, keeping your detailed finances private in public spaces.
- **History View**: Search and filter through your transaction history with ease.
- **Real-time Updates**: Your balance and stats update instantly as you manage your money.

### ☁️ Secure Cloud Backup
Sync your financial records securely with your Google Account, allowing you to restore your data whenever you switch devices.
